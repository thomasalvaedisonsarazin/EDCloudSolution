FAQ

Questions schools and shop owners ask

Does the software work in Tamil?

Yes. Screens and receipts can be in Tamil, English or both. The grocery POS has a one-tap Tamil/English switch.

Can I see a demo before I pay?

Yes. You get a free working demo, with your own items, students or menu loaded, before you pay anything.

Can you move my existing data from Excel?

Yes. Student lists, item lists and customer balances can be imported from Excel or CSV, so you don't start from zero.

What support do I get after installation?

Defects are fixed free for 30 days after go-live. After that, support and updates are covered by a yearly maintenance plan.

Which areas do you serve?

I'm based in Puducherry and work with schools and shops in Puducherry, Cuddalore, Villupuram, Chennai and the rest of Tamil Nadu. Demos can be in person or online.

Can you build software that isn't on this list?

Yes. Show me your current bill book, register or Excel sheet, and I'll tell you honestly whether custom software will save you time.
